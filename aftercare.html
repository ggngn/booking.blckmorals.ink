Aftercare

FOLLOW YOUR ARTIST'S AFTERCARE INSTRUCTIONS
  • Your artist knows how your tattoo was applied and what is best for your specific piece.
  • If you have questions or concerns during healing, reach out to your artist before changing your aftercare routine.
FIRST 3–4 DAYS — SECOND SKIN
  • Keep the second-skin bandage on for 3–4 days.
  • A small amount of blood, plasma, or ink underneath the bandage is normal.
  • Remove the bandage sooner if it becomes loose, water gets underneath it, or you develop significant irritation.
  • Ideally, remove the bandage toward the end of a shower to help loosen the adhesive.
  • Do not aggressively pull the bandage off while it is completely dry.
  • Slowly peel it back over itself rather than pulling upward.
AFTER REMOVING THE BANDAGE
  • Wash your hands before touching your tattoo.
  • Gently wash the tattoo with lukewarm water and mild, fragrance-free liquid soap.
  • Use only your clean fingertips.
  • Do not use bar soap, washcloths, loofahs, or sponges.
  • Do not scrub or exfoliate the tattoo.
  • Rinse thoroughly.
  • Gently pat dry with a clean paper towel — do not rub.
  • Allow the tattoo to air-dry briefly if needed.
MOISTURIZE
  • Apply a very thin layer of lightweight, fragrance-free lotion.
  • A little goes a long way.
  • Moisturize approximately 2–3 times daily or as needed.
  • Your tattoo should not look greasy, wet, or excessively shiny.
  • If using ointment, use only a very thin layer and preferably one recommended by your artist.
  • Avoid heavy layers of petroleum-based products.
WHAT'S NORMAL
  • Mild redness, swelling, tenderness, or warmth
  • Slight clear or tinted fluid
  • Itching
  • Dryness or tightness
  • Peeling or flaking
  • A dull, cloudy, or slightly uneven appearance while healing

DO NOT PICK, SCRATCH, OR PEEL YOUR TATTOO.

  • Let peeling skin fall away naturally.
  • If it's itchy, gently pat or tap the area instead of scratching.
SUN PROTECTION
  • Keep your healing tattoo out of direct sunlight.
  • Do not apply sunscreen to an unhealed tattoo.
  • Once completely healed, use SPF 30–50+ whenever your tattoo is exposed to the sun.
  • Sun exposure can cause tattoos to fade faster.
CLOTHING & FRICTION
  • Wear clean, loose-fitting clothing when possible.
  • Avoid tight clothing, straps, waistbands, or anything that repeatedly rubs against the tattoo.
  • Be especially mindful of friction during the first week.
WATER & SOAKING
  • Normal showers are okay.
  • Avoid excessively hot or prolonged showers.
  • Do NOT submerge your tattoo until it is fully healed.
  • Avoid baths.
  • Avoid swimming pools.
  • Avoid hot tubs / Jacuzzis.
  • Avoid lakes.
  • Avoid rivers.
  • Avoid oceans.
  • Avoid saunas and steam rooms.
EXERCISE
  • Be mindful of excessive sweating, friction, stretching, and contact with the tattoo.
  • Avoid workouts or equipment that repeatedly rub against the healing area.
  • Keep gym equipment and anything touching the tattoo clean.
SLEEPING
  • Keep your bedding clean while your tattoo is healing.
  • Avoid sleeping directly on the tattoo when possible.
  • If your tattoo sticks to fabric, do not pull it off dry.
  • Moisten the fabric with lukewarm water and gently release it.
PETS
  • Keep pets from licking, scratching, or rubbing against your tattoo.
  • Avoid direct contact with pet hair, saliva, and dirty pet bedding while your tattoo is healing.
TAKE CARE OF YOURSELF
  • Stay hydrated.
  • Eat regular, balanced meals.
  • Get enough rest.
  • Your body needs time and energy to heal your skin.
AVOID
  • Picking or scratching
  • Peeling the tattoo
  • Scrubbing or exfoliating
  • Fragranced lotions or harsh products
  • Heavy layers of moisturizer
  • Direct sunlight
  • Sunscreen on an unhealed tattoo
  • Swimming or soaking
  • Hot tubs and baths
  • Tight clothing or excessive friction
  • Letting pets lick or rub against the tattoo
  • Using unfamiliar products without checking with your artist
HEALING TIME
  • Most tattoos take approximately 2–4 weeks for the surface of the skin to heal.
  • Deeper layers can continue healing beyond this.
  • Your tattoo may look dull, cloudy, dry, or flaky during healing.
  • Don't judge the final appearance while your tattoo is still healing.
  • Be patient and allow your skin to heal at its own pace.
WHEN TO GET HELP
  • If something about your tattoo concerns you, contact your artist.
  • If symptoms become severe, unusual, or progressively worse, seek advice from a qualified medical professional.
  • Your artist can provide tattoo aftercare guidance but cannot diagnose medical conditions or infections.
THE GOLDEN RULES
  • KEEP IT CLEAN.
  • KEEP IT LIGHTLY MOISTURIZED.
  • DON'T PICK IT.
  • DON'T SOAK IT.
  • KEEP IT OUT OF THE SUN.
  • LET YOUR SKIN HEAL.
