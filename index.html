A Note About Healing

Every tattoo heals differently. Factors such as skin type, placement, sun exposure, aftercare, and individual healing can affect how a tattoo looks once settled.

Because of this, healed tattoos may appear softer, lighter, or slightly different from when they were freshly tattooed. This is completely normal.
