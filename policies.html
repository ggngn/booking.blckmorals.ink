Aftercare

✔ WHAT TO DO

  • Follow your artist's aftercare instructions.
  • Wash gently with mild, fragrance-free liquid soap and lukewarm water. No bar soap.
  • Apply a thin layer of recommended tattoo aftercare.
  • Protect from direct sunlight. Use SPF after healing.
  • Wear loose clothing.
  • Stay hydrated and eat healthy.
  • Avoid baths, pools and hot tubs.
  • Be patient. Healing usually takes 2–4 weeks.

✘ WHAT NOT TO DO

  • Don't scratch or pick at scabs.
  • Don't over-moisturize.
  • Don't expose healing tattoos to direct sunlight.
  • Avoid tight clothing.
  • Don't use alcohol or hydrogen peroxide.
  • Avoid strenuous activities and excessive sweating during healing.
